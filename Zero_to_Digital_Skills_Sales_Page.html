Questions

No. You can start on a phone with a free editor. A laptop makes longer projects easier but is not required.

Do I need any experience?

No. The kit starts from zero and explains each step in simple English.

Will I earn money?

No income or client is guaranteed. The kit helps you build skills, a portfolio and a professional approach. What you do with it is up to you.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Every project works with voiceover, text on screen, screen recording or your own footage.

Which software do I need?

Free tools such as CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, plus Canva. The guide keeps instructions general because menus change, and tells you where to check official help pages.

How long does it take?

The plan is 30 days at about 45 to 90 minutes a day. You can go slower and finish when you are ready.
